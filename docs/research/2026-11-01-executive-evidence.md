# Demand-planning decision brief: evidence ledger

Reviewed 2026-11-01. The brief frames an industry problem; it does not report customer outcomes from ChronoScope.

| Source | Claim admitted | Scope and limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Fildes, Goodwin and De Baets, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2024.07.006) | Approximately 147,000 forecasts from six studies; adjustments improved accuracy and bias for just over half of stock-keeping units, with differences between datasets. Positive adjustments were more likely to worsen performance. | Read the publisher's abstract, introduction and conclusions through the indexed full text. The DOI resolver itself returned 403 during the refresh. This is evidence about the included studies, not a population estimate of every organisation. |
| [Fildes and Goodwin, 2007](https://doi.org/10.1287/inte.1070.0309) | A survey of 149 forecasters and four case studies examined the use of management judgment; written reasons and outcome evaluation were recommended. | Read the publisher's abstract. The sample size is a study descriptor, not an industry cost estimate. |
| [Makridakis et al., M4](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2019.04.014) | The competition evaluated 100,000 series and motivates broad comparisons rather than local universal-winner claims. | DOI access returned 403; the count was corroborated at the [organiser's official page](https://www.unic.ac.cy/iff/research/forecasting/m-competitions/m4/). No M4 result is represented as a ChronoScope result. |
| [Hyndman and Koehler, 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2006.03.001) | Accuracy measures need careful interpretation across series; the atlas reports scaled and unscaled metrics. | The author's [paper](https://robjhyndman.com/papers/mase.pdf) and package documentation were refreshed. The historical seasonal scaling denominator is not a second method's error on the same final holdout. |
| [Ansari et al., Chronos-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15821) | The published model supports univariate, multivariate and covariate-informed forecasting. | Read the primary abstract; capability does not establish superiority for the atlas's selected cases. |
| [Gibbs and Candès, 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00170) | Adaptive conformal inference updates prediction sets when the distribution changes. | Read the primary abstract; its asymptotic coverage statement is not a finite-window guarantee for the stored trajectories. |

The cause weights of 40, 35 and 25 are the author's initial judgments of relative importance for organising the discussion. They are explicitly described as estimates in the brief's unknowns. They are not inferred causal shares, survey percentages or customer measurements. Local operational records must replace them before sizing a specific intervention.

All proposed actions are recommendations for a planning workflow, with role owners and checks. Their benefits have not been measured by this project. The three illustrations are explanatory warehouse scenes and contain no quantitative results.
